# GenHub - Your Complete Construction Management Solution

## What is GenHub?

GenHub is a modern construction project management platform designed specifically for general contractors, project managers, and construction teams. It brings all aspects of your construction projects into one easy-to-use application that works on your phone, tablet, or computer - even when you don't have internet access on the job site.

Think of GenHub as your digital command center for construction projects, replacing scattered emails, spreadsheets, text messages, and paper forms with one organized system everyone can access.

---

## Who is GenHub For?

- **General Contractors** - Manage multiple projects, bids, and subcontractors
- **Project Managers** - Track project progress, tasks, and daily operations
- **Field Workers** - Submit daily reports, track materials, and manage expenses
- **Subcontractors** - Receive bids, submit proposals, and collaborate on tasks
- **Clients** - Stay informed about project progress with real-time updates

---

## Key Features

### 1. **Project Management with Visual Timeline**

Track your construction projects from start to finish with an intuitive "subway map" style timeline that shows you exactly where each project stands.

**What you can do:**
- View all your projects in one place
- See which phase each project is in (Planning, Construction, Closeout, etc.)
- Monitor project health at a glance
- Click on any project to dive into the details

**Benefits:** Never lose track of where your projects stand. Instantly see what phase you're in and what comes next.

---

### 2. **Smart Task Management**

Organize all the work that needs to happen with flexible task boards that work the way you think.

**What you can do:**
- View tasks as cards on a board (Kanban style) or as a detailed list
- Drag and drop tasks between stages (To Do → In Progress → Done)
- Assign tasks to team members
- Set priorities and due dates
- Link materials and documents to specific tasks
- See which tasks are blocking others

**Benefits:** Everyone knows what they need to do, when it's due, and what materials they need. No more confusion about who's doing what.

---

### 3. **AI-Powered Bid Management**

Streamline your bidding process with smart tools that help you get competitive bids and award work faster.

**What you can do:**
- Create bid packages and automatically generate scope descriptions
- Invite subcontractors via email or text message
- Receive and compare all bids in one organized table
- Let AI highlight missing items and identify the best value
- Award work with one click (automatically creates tasks for the winning sub)

**Benefits:** Cut your bidding time in half. Get better bids because subs have all the information they need. Make smarter decisions with AI-powered comparisons.

---

### 4. **Materials Management (Home Depot Integration)**

Keep track of every material you need, from planning through installation.

**What you can do:**
- Search Home Depot's inventory directly in the app
- Check real-time prices and availability
- Add materials to specific tasks
- Upload receipts and let AI automatically match them to your materials
- Track material usage and costs across your entire project
- See which materials are assigned to which tasks

**Benefits:** Stop running to the store for forgotten materials. Know exactly what you've spent and what you still need. Receipt tracking means no more lost receipts at tax time.

---

### 5. **Team Communication**

Keep all your project conversations in one place, whether you're chatting with your team or clients.

**What you can do:**
- Chat rooms for each project and task
- Direct messages with team members
- Connect with KakaoTalk for seamless messaging
- Get notifications for important messages
- Share photos and documents in conversations

**Benefits:** Stop losing important information in text message threads. Everything is organized by project and saved for future reference.

---

### 6. **Expense Management**

Track every dollar spent on your projects with simple expense submission and approval.

**What you can do:**
- Submit expenses with receipt photos
- AI automatically reads receipts and fills in the details
- Managers can review and approve expenses
- Expenses automatically link to related tasks and materials
- Track spending against your budget

**Benefits:** Know exactly where your money is going. Faster reimbursements for your team. Better budget control for managers.

---

### 7. **Daily Site Reports**

Document each day's work with structured reports that keep everyone informed.

**What you can do:**
- Log weather conditions, crew details, and work completed
- Upload site photos
- Record safety notes and incidents
- Get AI-generated summaries (one for your team, one for the client)
- Automatically share summaries with clients

**Benefits:** Protect yourself with daily documentation. Keep clients happy with transparent updates. AI summaries save you 30 minutes per report.

---

### 8. **Project Analytics & Dashboards**

Get the big picture view of how your projects are performing.

**What you can do:**
- See all key metrics in visual dashboards
- Track budget vs. actual spending
- Monitor material usage across projects
- Evaluate subcontractor performance
- See days remaining and schedule health
- Drill down into details with one click

**Benefits:** Make data-driven decisions. Spot problems before they become expensive. Prove your value to clients with clear metrics.

---

### 9. **Client Portal**

Give your clients a window into their project without overwhelming them with details.

**What you can do (as a client):**
- View project timeline and progress
- Read AI-generated daily summaries
- Browse site photos organized by date
- Access project documents
- Review and approve change orders
- View invoices and payment history

**Benefits (for contractors):** Happy clients who feel informed. Fewer "how's it going?" phone calls. Professional image that wins you more work.

---

### 10. **Change Order Management**

Handle project changes professionally with AI-powered impact analysis.

**What you can do:**
- Create change orders with detailed descriptions
- AI predicts impact on cost and schedule
- Route to client for approval
- Approved changes automatically update budget and tasks

**Benefits:** No more scope creep. Clients see the impact before approving. Automated updates mean no manual spreadsheet changes.

---

### 11. **Team & Company Management**

Organize your workforce and manage subcontractor relationships.

**What you can do:**
- Invite team members and set their roles
- Manage subcontractor profiles and contact info
- Track subcontractor performance
- Set company settings and preferences
- Manage integrations (Home Depot, KakaoTalk, etc.)

**Benefits:** Everyone has the right level of access. Build a database of trusted subs. Onboard new team members in minutes.

---

### 12. **Works Anywhere - Even Offline**

GenHub is a Progressive Web App (PWA), which means it works like a native app on your phone or tablet, and keeps working even when you lose internet connection.

**What this means:**
- Install GenHub on your home screen like any other app
- Use it at job sites with poor cell service
- Changes sync automatically when you're back online
- No app store downloads or updates needed

**Benefits:** Never let poor connectivity slow you down. Work from the field just like you work from the office.

---

### 13. **Smart Notifications**

Stay informed without being overwhelmed.

**What you can do:**
- Get notified for important events (task assignments, bid submissions, approvals needed)
- Choose how you want to receive notifications (in-app, email, push notifications, KakaoTalk)
- Customize what you get notified about
- Click any notification to jump right to the relevant item

**Benefits:** Never miss critical deadlines. Stay in the loop without constantly checking the app.
