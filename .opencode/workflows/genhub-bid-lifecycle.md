# GenHub Bid Lifecycle Workflow

## Goal
Define, publish, and award subcontractor bids.

## Steps
1. Draft scope, trades, and requirements
2. Publish bid package and invite subs
3. Collect responses and compare
4. Award bid and convert to tasks/materials

## Command
- `/bid {scope}`

## Agents
- GenHubDomainOps
- GenHubProductUX

## Outputs
- Bid flow outline
- Schema/action checklist
- Edge cases and risks
